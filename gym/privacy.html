Peasy Gym – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026 · Italiano

In short

  • Peasy Gym has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your sessions, workouts and exercises stay on your phone.
  • We do not collect, see or sell your data. There are no ads, and the app has no analytics or tracking of its own.
  • The app does not connect to the internet by itself: its list of exercises is built in. Some data goes to Google only for payments and for Android's own backup if you have it turned on.

Who we are

Peasy Gym is made by Simone Bronzini, Italy. He has no access to the data in your app: it stays on your phone, under your control. He is responsible for the app's choices described on this page (which services the app talks to, and when) and for any email you send him. Contact: peasytools@gmail.com.

Data that stays on your phone

Everything you enter is stored only in the app's storage on your phone: your sessions and groups, your workouts (exercises, sets, weights, reps and times), the exercises you create or change, your weight unit, the app's settings, and the state of your free trial or subscription. We never receive any of it.

  • Exercise list: the list of exercises and their muscles is built into the app, so searching it happens on your phone. Nothing is sent.
  • Export files: when you export, the app writes a file where you choose. What happens to that file afterwards is up to you.
  • Deleting your data: uninstall the app, or clear its storage in your phone's settings. Delete any export files you made yourself.

Rest timer and notifications

During a workout the app shows a notification with your rest timer, also on the lock screen, and can vibrate when a rest is over. On Android 13 and later it asks for permission to show notifications when you start a workout. The notification is made on your phone and nothing is sent. You can hide it from the lock screen or turn it off in your phone's settings.

Payments

Subscriptions are sold and charged by Google Play. Your payment details go to Google, never to us. The app only learns from Google Play whether your subscription is active. Google handles payments under the Google Privacy Policy. You can manage or cancel your subscription in the Google Play app.

Android backup

If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may copy the app's data to your Google account, and restore it when you reinstall the app or set up a new phone. This is a feature of Android, run by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. We cannot see these backups. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

Crash and performance statistics

If you have allowed your phone to share usage and diagnostics data with Google, Google Play may give us statistics about crashes and app performance. They help us fix problems and do not include your workouts.

If you email us

If you write to us, we use your email address and message only to answer you, and we delete them when they are no longer needed.

Children

Peasy Gym is meant for adults. It is not directed at children.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, and to object to its use. Because we do not hold your data, there is usually nothing for us to give or delete: your data is on your phone, under your control. For data held by Google, contact Google through the link above. If you have questions, write to us. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority; in Italy this is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling data in a new way, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches the app.
