Peasy Food – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

In short

  • Peasy Food has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your diary, foods, recipes and plan stay on your phone.
  • We do not collect, see or sell your data. There are no ads, and the app has no analytics or tracking of its own.
  • Some data goes to other services only in these cases: Open Food Facts when you search online or look up a barcode; Google for payments, for the barcode reader's technical statistics, and for Android's own backup if you have it turned on.

Who we are

Peasy Food is made by Simone Bronzini, Italy. He has no access to the data in your app: it stays on your phone, under your control. He is responsible for the app's choices described on this page (which services the app talks to, and when) and for any email you send him. Contact: peasytools@gmail.com.

Data that stays on your phone

Everything you enter is stored only in the app's storage on your phone: your diary entries, the foods and recipes you create, your plan and targets, the app's settings, and the state of your free trial or subscription. We never receive any of it.

  • Export files: when you export, the app writes a file where you choose. What happens to that file afterwards is up to you.
  • QR codes: when you share a food, recipe or meal as a QR code, the code is shown on your screen. Nothing is sent over the internet.
  • Widgets: the home screen widgets read the same data on your phone.
  • Deleting your data: uninstall the app, or clear its storage in your phone's settings. Delete any export files you made yourself.

Open Food Facts (food search)

When you tap "Search online" or look up a barcode, the app sends your search words or the barcode number to Open Food Facts, a free, non-profit food database. The app never searches as you type: only when you ask. Like any internet request, Open Food Facts also receives your IP address and a technical label that names the app and our contact address (not yours). What you eat and your diary are never sent. Open Food Facts handles this under its own privacy policy.

Camera and barcode scanning

The camera is used only while you scan a barcode, and only if you allow it. The image is read on your phone by Google's ML Kit library; the images and the barcodes are not stored and not sent to anyone. According to Google, ML Kit "processing of the input data (e.g. images, video, text) fully happens on-device".

ML Kit does send Google some technical information to keep the library working: the device model and Android version, the app's name and version, performance figures such as speed, and settings such as the image size. Google states that this data is encrypted in transit, used for diagnostics and usage statistics, and not passed on to third parties. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure.

Payments

Subscriptions are sold and charged by Google Play. Your payment details go to Google, never to us. The app only learns from Google Play whether your subscription is active. Google handles payments under the Google Privacy Policy. You can manage or cancel your subscription in the Google Play app.

Android backup

If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may copy the app's data to your Google account, and restore it when you reinstall the app or set up a new phone. This is a feature of Android, run by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. We cannot see these backups. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

Crash and performance statistics

If you have allowed your phone to share usage and diagnostics data with Google, Google Play may give us statistics about crashes and app performance. They help us fix problems and do not include your diary.

If you email us

If you write to us, we use your email address and message only to answer you, and we delete them when they are no longer needed.

Children

Peasy Food is meant for adults. It is not directed at children.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, and to object to its use. Because we do not hold your data, there is usually nothing for us to give or delete: your data is on your phone, under your control. For data held by Open Food Facts or Google, contact them through the links above. If you have questions, write to us. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority; in Italy this is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling data in a new way, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches the app.
